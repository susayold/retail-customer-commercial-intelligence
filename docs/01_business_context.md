# Business context

## Audience

Retail commercial, CRM, customer analytics and BI stakeholders.

## Business objective

Turn observed panel transactions and marketing activity into trusted measures, diagnosis and actions. The project follows the chain:

customer engagement → basket behavior → category/brand → promotion → campaign/coupon → decision support.

## Questions by layer

1. Customer: Which observed households increase or decrease spend? Is movement frequency-led or basket-value-led?
2. Basket: What is a typical observed basket? Which categories attach?
3. Category/brand: Which departments and commodities are broad, deep, private-label heavy or deteriorating?
4. Promotion: Which display/mailer states are associated with stronger panel activity?
5. Campaign/coupon: Which recipient groups redeem and show observed repeat behavior?
6. Decision: Which action should be tested or monitored next?

## Scope boundaries

- The panel is not the full retailer customer population.
- DAY/WEEK_NO are observation indexes, not calendar dates.
- First observed purchase is not customer acquisition.
- Campaign recipients may be targeted, so recipients versus non-recipients is not automatically lift.
- Promotion exposure is observational; use associated with, not caused.
- No COGS, margin, campaign cost or inventory fields exist.

## Stakeholder deliverables

- trusted source inventory and data-quality report;
- governed KPI dictionary;
- customer, basket, category, promotion and campaign marts;
- three root-cause case studies;
- six-page Power BI decision surface;
- SQL-to-DAX reconciliation and UAT;
- at least five evidence-backed decisions after the real data run.
